FIRESTICK-WII — ONE REPOSITORY / WINDOWS 10

1. Connect the Fire Stick's HDMI output to a TV/monitor (not a PC HDMI port).
   The PC's Micro-USB may power the Stick but may not enable ADB.
   The MT65xx Preloader driver does NOT replace Android ADB.
2. Ensure it boots, and enable/authorize ADB using your remote or mobile app:
   Settings > My Fire TV > Developer Options > ADB Debugging = ON.
3. Prebuilt launcher APK is ready in dist/firestick-wii-launcher.apk.
   If you wish to recompile locally, JDK 21 / Android SDK 34 are supported.
4. Run the automated test suite anytime (no hardware needed):
   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\scripts\test-suite.ps1
5. To start the guided setup, double-click run.bat (USB ADB), or run:
   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\setup.ps1 -FireTvIp 192.168.1.50
   (Replace 192.168.1.50 with your Stick's actual local IP address).
6. Follow prompts to test TV Bro, install official AirScreen from Amazon Appstore,
   test iPhone AirPlay and Pixel casting, and test the Wii launcher.
7. Optional: approve a reversible debloat plan after reviewing it.
8. HOME replacement may be blocked by modern Fire OS. Try only after manual validation:
   powershell -File .\scripts\activate-home.ps1 -FireTvIp YOUR_IP
9. Rollback anytime (restores only packages modified by this toolkit):
   powershell -File .\scripts\restore.ps1 -FireTvIp YOUR_IP

See README.md and reports/TEST-REPORT.md for full technical details.
